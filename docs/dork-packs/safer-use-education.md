## 👥 About This Pack

Safer-use education is strongest when it's peer-produced. Lead with **NUAA** (the _User's News_ and
_Insider's News_ magazines, **PeerLink**) and **DanceWize** crowd-care resources, then the Alcohol
and Drug Foundation's plain-language **Drug Facts**. For blood-borne viruses there are named bodies
(**Hepatitis Australia**, **Hepatitis NSW**, **ASHM**); for multilingual material, Victoria's
**Health Translations**. Internationally, **DanceSafe**, **TripSit**, **Erowid**, **The Loop**,
**Crew** and NZ's **The Level** carry decades of plain-language know-how.

> **Entity reference:** every producer and series below is catalogued in
> [Source Intelligence → Safer-Use Education](../resources/source-intelligence.md#safer-use-education-plain-language-resources).

## 🟢 Basic Queries

### Peer-Produced Resources (NUAA / DanceWize)

```txt
(site:nuaa.org.au OR site:usersnews.com.au OR site:hrvic.org.au OR site:dancewizensw.org.au) ("User's News" OR "Insider's News" OR "DanceWize" OR factsheet)
```

**Why this works:**

- NUAA's _User's News_ / _Insider's News_ and DanceWize's crowd-care material are written by and for
  people who use drugs — the by-and-with-peers core of safer-use education

### ADF Plain-Language Drug Info

```txt
site:adf.org.au ("Drug Facts" OR "Power of Words" OR "safer use")
```

```txt

```
