# Sex Worker Health & Safety

> Health, safety, and harm reduction by and for sex workers — led by the peer organisations, named
> by the actual org and resource, with decriminalisation status that shapes the framing.

---

> **Rights-based framing.** Sex work is work. This pack centres worker-led knowledge and autonomy.
> Use **"sex worker" / "sex work"** (never "prostitute" except when citing legislation).
> **Decriminalisation ≠ legalisation** — they mean different things; sex-worker orgs back full
> decriminalisation. Decrim status drives framing: **NSW** (1995), **NT** (2019), **VIC** (Dec
> 2023), **QLD** (Aug 2024) decriminalised; **SA is still fully criminalised** — frame SA for legal
> risk.
>
> **Entity reference:** every peer org and resource below is catalogued in
> [Source Intelligence → Sex Worker Health (Peer-Led)](../resources/source-intelligence.md#sex-worker-health-peer-led).

## 🟢 Basic Queries

### Peer Peak & Health Resource (Scarlet Alliance)

```txt
site:scarletalliance.org.au ("Red Book" OR "migration project" OR SANTP OR drug OR "harm reduction")
```

**Why this works:**

- Goes to the national peer body and its named resource (**Red Book** — sexual-health info "by sex
  workers, for sex workers") rather than a generic `"sex worker" health` query

### State Peer Organisations

```txt
(site:swop.org.au OR site:vixen.org.au OR site:respectqld.org.au OR site:sin.org.au OR site:magenta.org.au) (drug OR alcohol OR "harm reduction" OR health OR safety)
```

**Why this works:**

- Names the peer-led state orgs — SWOP (NSW), Vixen (VIC, peer-only), Respect Inc (QLD), SIN (SA),
  Magenta (WA). Note Vixen is `vixen.org.au` (not vixencollective.org)

### Global Peer Network (NSWP)

```txt
site:nswp.org ("smart guide" OR "briefing paper" OR "community guide") (drug OR "harm reduction")
```
